NOTE: a self-signed TLS certificate was generated for this release. Clients must
trust it (or use -k / insecure) — supply your own via tls.existingSecret or
tls.cert/tls.key for production.

To connect a swamp CLI client (e.g. `swamp datastore status --server https://...`),
trust the generated CA via DENO_CERT:

   kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get secret {{ include "swamp-serve.tlsSecretName" . }} \
     -o jsonpath='{.data.ca\.crt}' | base64 -d > swamp-ca.crt
   export DENO_CERT="$PWD/swamp-ca.crt"
